### **Updated Breakdown of the Home Page (Wireframe)**  

The **Home Page** provides an overview of the portal and quick access to key features.

---

### **1. Sidebar Navigation**
- **Portal Logo & Title:** Displays "Self-Service Portal."
- **Navigation Menu:** Links to key sections:
  - **Home**
  - **Request Access**
  - **Approval Status**
  - **Help/FAQs**
  - **Admin Dashboard**

---

### **2. Header Section**
- **User Profile Menu** (Top-right, includes settings, logout, and dark mode toggle)
- **Notifications Icon** (Bell icon to show pending approvals or request updates)

---

### **3. Welcome Section**
- **Title:** "Welcome to the Self-Service Portal"
- **Brief Description:** Explains the purpose of the portal (employees can request access to specific URLs, which go through multiple approval stages).
- **Personalized Greeting:** E.g., "Hello, [User’s Name], you have **X** pending approvals."

---

### **4. Quick Actions Panel**
- **Buttons for Essential Actions:**
  - **"Request Access"** (Navigates to the Request Access Page)
  - **"Track Your Requests"** (Navigates to Approval Status Page)
  - **"View Approved Rules"** (Lists previous approvals)
  - **"Help & FAQs"** (Navigates to user support resources)

---

### **5. Dashboard Overview (For Users with Permissions)**
- **Displays key metrics:**
  - **Requests Made:** Total number of access requests submitted.
  - **Pending Approvals:** Requests waiting for approval.
  - **Approved Requests:** Successfully granted requests.
  - **Recent Activity Log:** Displays recent actions (submissions, approvals, denials).

---

### **6. Announcements & Updates Section**
- **Portal Updates:** Displays important notices about changes, new features, or security updates.
- **Security Notices:** Highlights essential compliance reminders.

---

### **7. Footer Section**
- **Company Information**
- **Support & Contact Links**
- **Privacy Policy & Terms of Service**

---

This layout ensures a **clean, modern, and structured** user experience with easy access to essential portal features. Let me know if you'd like any adjustments! 🚀
