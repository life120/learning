### **Updated Breakdown of the Login Page**  

The **Login Page** is designed for **secure and user-friendly authentication** into the **Self-Service Portal**.

---

### **1. Login Form Container**  
- **Centered Login Box** with **responsive design** for various screen sizes.  
- **Company Logo & Portal Name:** Displayed at the top for branding.  

---

### **2. Login Form Section**  
- **Title:** "Sign In to Self-Service Portal"  
- **Input Fields:**  
  - **Email or Username:** Users enter their registered email or username.  
  - **Password:** Secure password input field with an option to toggle visibility (can be added later).  

- **Login Button:**  
  - **Primary button** to submit credentials and authenticate the user.  

---

### **3. Remember Me & Forgot Password Section**  
- **Remember Me Checkbox:**  
  - Users can select this option to stay logged in on their device.  
  - **Checkbox and Label are Aligned in One Line** for better UX.  

- **Forgot Password Link:**  
  - Redirects users to the password recovery page.  

---

### **4. Error Handling & Notifications**  
- **Inline Error Messages:** Displayed if login credentials are incorrect.  
- **Account Lock Alerts:** Shown if multiple failed login attempts occur.  

---

### **5. Footer Section**  
- **Company Support & Contact Links**  
- **Privacy Policy & Terms of Service**  

---

### **Dynamic Features**  
- **Password Visibility Toggle (Future Enhancement):** Allows users to see/hide password input.  
- **Real-Time Validation:** Checks if fields are filled before enabling the login button.  
- **Dark Mode Support (Future Enhancement):** Adjusts UI for users who prefer a dark mode experience.
