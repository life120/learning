### **Help/FAQs Page - Breakdown of Sections**  

The **Help/FAQs Page** will serve as a support hub where users can find answers to common questions and get assistance with using the portal.

---

### **1. Sidebar Navigation**  
- **Portal Logo & Title:** Displays "Self-Service Portal."  
- **Navigation Menu:**  
  - Home  
  - Request Access  
  - Approval Status  
  - **Help/FAQs (Current Page Highlighted)**  
  - Admin Dashboard  

---

### **2. Header Section**  
- **Title:** "Help & FAQs"  
- **Brief Description:** A short introduction explaining that users can find answers to frequently asked questions and request further assistance if needed.  
- **User Profile Menu** (Top-right, includes settings, logout, and dark mode toggle)  

---

### **3. Search Bar**  
- Allows users to **search FAQs** by entering a keyword.  

---

### **4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) Section**  
- **Collapsible FAQ List:** Questions appear as clickable items, and answers expand when clicked.  
- **Example FAQs:**  
  1. **How do I request access to a URL?**  
  2. **How long does the approval process take?**  
  3. **What should I do if my request is rejected?**  
  4. **Who are the approvers in my request workflow?**  
  5. **How do I check my request status?**  
  6. **How can I cancel my request?**  

---

### **5. Contact Support Section**  
- **Contact Form** for users who need additional help.  
- **Fields:**  
  - Name  
  - Email  
  - Issue Description (Textarea)  
  - **Submit Button** (Sends inquiry to support team)  

---

### **6. Additional Resources Section**  
- **Links to External Documentation:** Guides or policies related to access requests.  
- **Live Chat Button (Optional):** For real-time support if available.  

---

### **7. Footer Section**  
- **Company Information**  
- **Support & Contact Links**  
- **Privacy Policy & Terms of Service**  

---

### **Dynamic Features**  
- **Collapsible FAQ Answers:** Clicking a question expands its answer.  
- **Search Bar Auto-Filters FAQs:** Displays only relevant questions based on the input.  
- **Contact Form Validation:** Ensures required fields are filled before submission.
