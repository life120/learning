1. Create your unique idea of success
You need to define what their unique idea of a rich life and success is.
Why did you start a business?
Things will get tough, you will need a big aspirational vision to keep you going. 
Write down what success looks like
e.g. Impact + Profit + Lifestyle
Impact: I want to reach out to as many people as possible. So the focus will not go to one-on-one coaching, but activities that will reach out to as much people as possible. 
Profit: Make sure it is profitable
Lifestyle: I want to be able to travel 2 - 3 weeks at a time for some other stuff and not working on it all day long.
As you pursue going into business, you need to ask yourself:
- What is the purpose of me doing this?
- What do I get from doing all this?

You need to clearly define your definition of success.

You should not be living the same way as before. Once you create your own definition of your own rich life, then the lifestyle choices that you make needs to progress towards that.
